Pirate ship - sailing simulation

Double-click "Play - High (RTX).bat".
If it fails or looks wrong, try "Play - Low (fallback).bat".
If a window does not appear, run godot_console.exe --path game from a terminal to see errors.

On deck: WASD walk, mouse look, Shift hurry, C crouch, hold Q to brace against the ship's motion,
Space jump / let go of a rope, E interact (hold for compass), Esc to release the mouse.
The deck moves under you: you walk slower uphill, stagger when she lurches, and slide on a wet deck.
Hold Q in heavy weather or a knockdown will throw you across the deck.

Weather: press Tab for the weather menu. Sliders for wind, gusts, fetch, swell, cloud, rain, fog and
lightning; presets Calm, Fair, Fresh, Gale and Storm; "Build the sea now"; "Send a rogue wave".
The weather eases toward what you set, and with "Weather changes on its own" fronts come through by themselves.

Sailing: climb to the poop deck (the highest deck at the stern), look at the ship's wheel and press E to take the helm.
At the helm: A/D steer (port/starboard), W/S set or take in sail (furled, reefed, working, full), E to step away.
The ship sails by the wind: it cannot sail closer than about 60 degrees to it, is fastest with the wind on the quarter,
and only answers the rudder while it has way on. Too much sail in a blow and she is overpressed: reef (S).

Heavy weather: run before a big sea and she surfs; let the sea get on her quarter and she broaches.
In seas over 3.5 m the lookout will call rogue waves with their bearing. Turn her bow into them.
Beam-on, she is knocked down; from astern, she is pooped. The ship's log in the weather menu keeps count.
